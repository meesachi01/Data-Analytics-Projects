# Olist Marketplace EDA — Capstone Project

Exploratory data analysis of **Olist**, one of Brazil's largest e-commerce marketplaces, covering **September 2016 – August 2018**. Data was extracted with SQL in **Google BigQuery** and visualized in an interactive **Tableau dashboard** to answer key business questions on revenue, delivery performance, and the seller ecosystem.

## 📂 Repository Contents

| File | Description |
|------|-------------|

| `Seller_Segmentation.sql` | RFM-based seller segmentation query |

## 🔑 Key Findings

- **R$ 15.49M** total revenue from **96,478 orders** — revenue grew **~244%** year-over-year
- Average delivery time dropped from **54 days to 2 days**; **91.9%** of orders delivered on time
- **Pareto pattern everywhere:** top 8 states ≈ 81% of revenue (São Paulo alone ~33%); top 15 product categories ≈ 81% of revenue
- **412 Top Sellers** (14% of the base) generate **over half of all revenue** (R$ 8.33M)
- Average customer rating of **4.08 / 5**, supported by improving delivery performance
